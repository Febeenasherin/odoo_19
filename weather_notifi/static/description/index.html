How It Works

1. Browse Records

Open any record (Sales Order, Contact, Product, Invoice) in Odoo form view. The module automatically logs your visit.

2. Open Systray Dropdown

Click the history clock icon in the top right systray bar to view your last 20 visited records with record names and model labels.

3. 1-Click Reopen

Click any item in the dropdown or User history tab to instantly open the record form view in Odoo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions buyers usually ask before installation.

How does the module track recently viewed records?

Whenever you open a form view in Odoo, the module automatically logs the record model, ID, and display name for your user account.

Where can I see my recently viewed records?

Click the history clock icon in the top bar (systray) or open your User profile form view under Settings > Users to view the 'Recently Viewed History' tab.

How many records are kept in history?

Up to 20 records per user are stored server-side and automatically updated on each visit.

Can I clear my viewing history?

Yes! Click the 'Clear History' button at the bottom of the systray dropdown to clear your history entries.
